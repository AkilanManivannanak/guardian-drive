Physics-based sensor simulation makes the classifier robust to noise it never saw

Under 0 dB real electrode-motion and muscle noise, AUROC rises from 0.654 to 0.818 with the sensor model, which beats training on the real recorded noise itself. Adding diffusion samples gives the best result everywhere. Mean ± std over 3 seeds.

40 % of diffusion samples were broken; the cause was one number in the noise schedule

Guidance was the obvious suspect, but samples still saturated at w = 0. DDIM started where the cosine schedule leaves ᾱ ≈ 2.4×10⁻⁹, multiplying prediction error ~20,000×. Starting one step earlier, plus per-sample thresholding, fixed it with no retraining. Selected on the validation fold.

A verified electro-mechanical model of the electrode on skin

Contact mechanics is coupled to an electro-quasi-static finite-volume solve in layered tissue, verified to 2×10⁻⁴ against an analytic solution. Drag the slider to lift one electrode off the skin.

Physics priors win only when noise dominates

On real ECG with real noise, the physics-constrained fits (hard and PINN) beat plain filtering at 0 dB. At higher SNR, a five-event single-template ODE cannot represent all real morphology, so filtering wins. Output SNR in dB on 16 records.
